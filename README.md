# 1M -- Bullworth Academy Online

Online multiplayer for *Bully: Scholarship Edition*.

## Requirements

- Windows
- *Bully: Scholarship Edition* on **Steam**, owned on your Steam account, with Steam running

## How to play

1. Download **`1MLauncher.exe`** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Run it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Press **PLAY** on the title menu.

Hold **Shift** while starting the launcher for options (check setup, restore your save,
back up your account, uninstall).

## Help

Send `%LOCALAPPDATA%\1M\launcher.log` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\1M\game\client.log`.
